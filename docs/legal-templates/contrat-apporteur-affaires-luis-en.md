## RECITALS

Lucid-Lab is an independent agency specialising in the design, development and integration of artificial intelligence agents, business automations, knowledge systems (the installation of Claude and Obsidian, referred to as the "Claude + Obsidian setup"), websites and content, for small and mid-sized companies.

The Introducer has a professional network and wishes to introduce to Lucid-Lab prospects likely to purchase these services. He is also employed full time in the United States and acts under this Agreement solely outside that employment, on a strictly independent basis.

## Article 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Agreement is to define the terms on which the Introducer introduces new business to Lucid-Lab, qualifies it and supports it through to signature, in return for a commission calculated on the revenue actually collected by Lucid-Lab.

The Introducer acts as an independent business introducer. Lucid-Lab retains the entire contractual relationship with the client, as well as delivery, quality and after-sales service, subject to Article 8.

---

## Article 2. Status of the Introducer

### 2.1 Independence

The Introducer carries out his activity in full independence. He freely organises his time, his methods and his means. He is subject to no working hours, no standing instructions, no binding targets and no disciplinary authority of Lucid-Lab. He bears his own operating costs.

This Agreement creates no relationship of subordination, no employment contract, no de facto partnership, no joint venture and no general power of representation.

### 2.2 Express exclusion of commercial agent status

The Introducer has no power to negotiate, conclude, amend or terminate any contract in the name of or on behalf of Lucid-Lab. He receives no permanent mandate to negotiate within the meaning of Article L. 134-1 of the French Commercial Code.

All contracts are negotiated, drawn up and signed by Lucid-Lab directly with the client. Prices, scope, deadlines and general terms are decided by Lucid-Lab alone.

The Parties therefore agree that commercial agent status (Articles L. 134-1 et seq. of the French Commercial Code) is expressly excluded, together with the termination indemnity provided for in Article L. 134-12 of that Code. The Introducer shall refrain from any conduct inconsistent with this qualification, and in particular from negotiating prices or scope outside the framework set out in Article 9.

### 2.3 No exclusivity

This Agreement is not exclusive on either side. The Introducer remains free to carry out any other activity, including business introduction for third parties, subject to Article 13. Lucid-Lab remains free to use other introducers and other sales channels, and to prospect directly.

No exclusivity may be requested from the Introducer without separate negotiation and specific consideration, formalised by a written amendment.

### 2.4 The Introducer's employment

The Introducer warrants that his employment contract and his employer's internal rules allow him to carry out the activity contemplated in this Agreement. He shall provide that confirmation to Lucid-Lab before signature.

His employment takes precedence. The Introducer shall not:

- approach the clients or prospects of his employer;
- use the time, tools, premises, data or confidential information of his employer;
- perform this Agreement in any situation where doing so would conflict with his employment obligations.

Any provision of this Agreement shall be deemed unwritten to the exact extent that it would conflict with the Introducer's employment contract.

### 2.5 Charges and taxes

The Introducer is solely responsible for all filings, contributions and taxes due in his country of residence and in any other relevant jurisdiction. He declares that he complies with his professional and tax obligations.

As the Introducer is established outside the European Union, his invoices are issued excluding VAT. Value added tax is, where applicable, self-assessed by Lucid-Lab under the reverse charge mechanism pursuant to Articles 259-1 and 283-2 of the French Tax Code. Any withholding tax applicable in the Introducer's country of residence remains at his expense.

The Introducer shall indemnify Lucid-Lab against any consequence, in particular financial, of a reclassification of this relationship as an employment contract or a commercial agency contract resulting from an inaccurate statement on his part or from a breach of Articles 2.1, 2.2 and 9.

## Article 3. Qualified business and trigger for commission

### 3.1 Definition of qualified business

"**Qualified Business**" means business for which the Introducer has gathered and documented, cumulatively:

1. **the need**: identification and written description of the prospect's actual need, of the problem to be solved and of the contemplated scope;
2. **the budget**: identification of a budget envelope confirmed by the prospect and consistent with Lucid-Lab's price list;
3. **the decision-maker**: identification by name of the person with signing authority, and effective access to that person;
4. **attendance**: the Introducer's attendance at meetings, from qualification through to signature, including the meeting at which the commercial proposal is presented, unless Lucid-Lab agrees otherwise in writing.

### 3.2 Trigger

Commission is due for Qualified Business carried through to signature. It arises upon signature of the client contract and becomes payable as and when amounts are collected, in accordance with Articles 6 and 7.

### 3.3 A mere introduction of a contact creates no entitlement

Providing a name, an email address, an introduction or a referral does not constitute Qualified Business and creates no entitlement to commission, whatever the commercial outcome.

The Parties may agree in writing, case by case and **before the first meeting**, on a reduced introduction fee for a simple referral. In the absence of such prior written agreement, no introduction fee is due.

### 3.4 Lucid-Lab's freedom to contract

Lucid-Lab remains free to decline any business, without having to give reasons, in particular for reasons of delivery capacity, risk, conflict of interest, prospect solvency or compliance. A refusal creates no entitlement to commission or to any indemnity.

## Article 4. Account registration and attribution

### 4.1 Prior registration

Before any approach, the Introducer registers the account with Lucid-Lab in writing (by email to the dedicated address provided by Lucid-Lab), stating the company name, the contact name and the anticipated need.

### 4.2 Lucid-Lab's response

Lucid-Lab replies within five (5) business days. It accepts the registration, or rejects it if the account is already a client, already in discussion, already registered by a third party, or excluded for another reason. Failure to reply within that period constitutes acceptance.

### 4.3 Protection

Acceptance of the registration grants the Introducer **six (6) months** of protection on the account concerned, from the date of acceptance. During that period, any business signed with that account gives rise to commission under the conditions of Article 3.

Protection may be extended by written agreement, upon evidence of active and documented pursuit of the sales cycle. Failing extension, protection expires automatically and without formality.

### 4.4 Excluded accounts

The following are excluded from registration and from any commission: Lucid-Lab's existing clients, prospects already recorded in its CRM at the date of registration, accounts generated by its own campaigns, and the clients and prospects of the Introducer's employer (Article 2.4).

### 4.5 Attribution disputes

In the event of a dispute over the origin of a piece of business, attribution goes to the Party that demonstrates it generated the first qualified meeting with the prospect, with written evidence (email, calendar invitation, meeting notes).

## Article 5. Commission rates

Rates depend on the **type of service sold**, not on how long the account has been a client.

### 5.1 Claude + Obsidian setup: 30 %

The rate is **thirty per cent (30 %)** for the Claude + Obsidian setup, that is, the installation and commissioning of the knowledge system supported by Anthropic's Claude assistant and by Obsidian, as standardised by Lucid-Lab (approximately two man-days, scope and deliverables defined in the standard commercial proposal).

For the avoidance of doubt, this refers to **Claude**, the artificial intelligence assistant published by Anthropic. Any reference to "Cloud" would be a clerical error with no effect.

### 5.2 All other services: 15 %

The rate is **fifteen per cent (15 %)** for all other services, in particular: audits, AI roadmaps, development and integration work, automations, websites, training, support engagements and recurring subscriptions.

This lower rate reflects the fact that delivery of these services may overrun and absorb Lucid-Lab's margin.

### 5.3 Mixed engagements

Where a single piece of business covers several types of service, commission is calculated line by line, on the breakdown set out in the client's order form or invoice. In the absence of a breakdown, the 15 % rate applies to the whole amount.

### 5.4 Reference rate

The Parties agree that **the reference rate of this Agreement is 15 %**. This definition is used in Article 11.4 (substitution).

### 5.5 Duration of commission on recurring revenue

For recurring services (subscriptions, maintenance, monthly support), commission is due on each amount collected for the entire term of the client contract, subject to Article 15 if this Agreement terminates.

## Article 6. Commission base

### 6.1 Principle

Commission is calculated on the **revenue, excluding taxes, actually collected** by Lucid-Lab in respect of the business concerned.

Neither invoiced revenue, nor revenue including taxes, nor signed but uncollected orders form the commission base.

### 6.2 Exclusions

The following are excluded from the base:

- value added tax and any other collected tax;
- third-party costs rebilled to the client, in particular AI consumption (Anthropic, OpenAI and other tokens), advertising budgets, third-party tool subscriptions and licences, domain names and hosting, travel expenses and services subcontracted at the client's request;
- discounts, credit notes and commercial gestures, commission being calculated on the amount actually collected;
- amounts not recovered, including in the event of non-payment, insolvency proceedings against the client, or dispute.

### 6.3 Adjustment

In the event of a credit note, refund to the client, cancellation, chargeback or partial recovery after a commission has been paid, the corresponding commission is adjusted. The adjustment is set off first against subsequent commissions. Failing sufficient commissions within six (6) months, the Introducer shall repay the excess within thirty (30) days of a written request.

This adjustment of the base is distinct from any clawback in the event of a client leaving, which is excluded by Article 7.4.

## Article 7. Payment terms

### 7.1 Single payment upon collection

Each commission is paid **in a single payment**, within **ten (10) days** of Lucid-Lab actually collecting the corresponding amount.

Commission is never spread over monthly instalments. Where the client pays in several instalments, each collection gives rise to payment of the corresponding commission in full, within the same ten (10) day period.

### 7.2 Invoicing and statement

Lucid-Lab provides the Introducer with a statement showing, for each collection: the client, the invoice reference, the amount collected excluding taxes, the collection date, the applicable rate and the commission amount.

The Introducer issues his invoice on the basis of that statement. The ten (10) day period provided for in Article 7.1 runs from the collection date, subject to receipt of a compliant invoice.

### 7.3 Currency and costs

The reference currency of this Agreement is the **euro (EUR)**. Commissions are calculated in euros on the amounts collected by Lucid-Lab.

Payments are made in euros by bank transfer. If the Introducer requests payment in another currency, conversion is made at the European Central Bank reference rate on the payment date.

Transfer issuance fees are borne by Lucid-Lab. **Receiving fees, intermediary bank fees and all foreign exchange costs and losses are borne by the Introducer.** Lucid-Lab bears no currency risk.

### 7.4 No clawback when a client leaves

If a client stops purchasing or terminates, commissions already paid remain definitively acquired by the Introducer. Future payments simply cease, with no clawback and no penalty, subject only to Article 6.3.

### 7.5 Supporting information

Once per calendar year, the Introducer may request in writing a reconciliation between registered accounts, amounts collected and commissions paid. Lucid-Lab shall respond within thirty (30) days. This right grants no access to Lucid-Lab's accounts, contracts or client data.

## Article 8. Delivery by the Introducer under Lucid-Lab's supervision

### 8.1 Principle

Where the Introducer wishes to deliver all or part of a Claude + Obsidian setup himself, and Lucid-Lab agrees, that delivery is governed by a **prior written mission order**, signed by both Parties, on a mission-by-mission basis. The Introducer acquires no right to deliver.

### 8.2 Man-day split

The mission order sets the total number of man-days for the engagement and their allocation between the Parties.

By way of illustration, on a setup engagement assessed at two (2) man-days, the reference split is **one point two (1.2) days for the Introducer and zero point eight (0.8) days for Lucid-Lab**, the latter share covering in particular supervision, quality control and security.

### 8.3 Payment for days delivered

The man-days performed by the Introducer are paid at the daily rate set out in the mission order. This payment is in addition to, and does not replace, the introduction commission provided for in Article 5.

It is invoiced by the Introducer as an independent subcontractor, after acceptance of the delivery by Lucid-Lab, and paid in accordance with Article 7.1.

### 8.4 Supervision, quality and security

Supervision of the engagement, quality control, security decisions and compliance remain at all times the responsibility of Lucid-Lab.

The Introducer performs the mission in accordance with Lucid-Lab's procedures, templates and security rules. He shall carry out no installation, no access and no data processing beyond the scope of the mission order. He retains no client data after the mission.

The client remains Lucid-Lab's client. Lucid-Lab remains solely responsible to the client for the engagement.

### 8.5 Take-back of delivery

Lucid-Lab may at any time take back delivery where quality, security, deadlines or client satisfaction so require. In that case, only the days actually performed and accepted are due to the Introducer. The introduction commission provided for in Article 5 is unaffected.

## Article 11. Prospect of an equity stake

### 11.1 No commitment

This Article opens a prospect. **It constitutes neither a promise to issue or transfer shares, nor an option, nor a right to enter the share capital, nor an undertaking to negotiate on any determined terms.**

### 11.2 Minimum length of relationship

No share allocation may be considered before **one (1) year** of continuous relationship under this Agreement, counted from its effective date, and subject to loyal and effective performance.

### 11.3 Unanimous approval of the three shareholders

Any allocation of shares, in whatever form, is **conditional upon the prior unanimous written approval of Lucid-Lab's three shareholders**: Mr Jules Gouron, Mr Théo Bénard and Periscope-X SARL.

No shareholder, director or representative may commit the Company's share capital alone. Any statement to the contrary is deemed unwritten and of no effect.

### 11.4 Substitution, never accumulation

The reference rate of this Agreement is 15 % (Article 5.4). Any amount paid to the Introducer under a rate above 15 %, being at this date the fifteen (15) additional points applicable to the Claude + Obsidian setup, constitutes an **advance to be set off against any future allocation of shares**.

Lucid-Lab keeps a cumulative record of those amounts (the "**Substitution Account**"). In the event of a share allocation, the value of the Substitution Account is deducted from the value of the shares allocated, using the valuation method set out in Article 11.6.

Under no circumstances may the Introducer combine a commission above the reference rate with an unreduced allocation of shares.

### 11.5 Vesting

Any allocation would be subject to vesting over **four (4) years with an initial twelve (12) month cliff**, monthly or quarterly vesting thereafter, and leaver provisions distinguishing good leavers from bad leavers, the latter forfeiting unvested shares and being subject to a buy-back right over vested shares.

### 11.6 Valuation

The valuation method used as the basis for any allocation is **agreed in writing before any discussion on equity is opened**, and not at the time of that discussion. In the absence of a written agreed method, no allocation may take place.

### 11.7 Receiving structure

The Parties agree that the structure hosting any equity stake is not necessarily Lucid-Lab SAS. It may be a **local subsidiary yet to be incorporated**, in particular in the Introducer's country of residence, the chosen structure being decided by the three shareholders in light of the legal, tax and administrative constraints applicable to a non-resident shareholder.

### 11.8 No vested right

The absence of any share allocation, for whatever reason, including the achievement of a performance threshold, constitutes neither a fault, nor a breach, nor a ground for termination, and creates no entitlement to any indemnity for the Introducer.

## Article 15. Term, termination and commission tail

### 15.1 Term

This Agreement takes effect on the date of signature by the last of the Parties and is entered into for an indefinite term.

### 15.2 Ordinary termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, without cause and without indemnity, subject to **thirty (30) days' written notice**, given by email with acknowledgement of receipt or by registered letter.

### 15.3 Termination for breach

In the event of a material breach not remedied within fifteen (15) days of a formal notice remaining without effect, either Party may terminate this Agreement automatically, without notice and without indemnity.

Material breaches by the Introducer include in particular: breach of Articles 2.2, 2.4, 9.2, 12, 13 or 14, a misleading statement to a prospect, use of unlawfully obtained data, and any conduct damaging Lucid-Lab's reputation.

### 15.4 Commission tail

In the event of ordinary termination, the Introducer receives the commissions due on amounts collected by Lucid-Lab during the **six (6) months** following the effective date of termination, in respect only of business signed before that date. After six months, no further commission is due, whatever the continuation of the client relationship.

Business registered but not signed at the effective date of termination gives rise to no commission, unless signature occurs within thirty (30) days of that date.

Lucid-Lab may, at its own initiative, discharge the commission tail early by paying a single amount equal to **four (4) times the monthly average of the commissions paid over the three (3) months** preceding termination. That payment definitively extinguishes any subsequent right to commission.

### 15.5 No tail in the event of breach

In the event of termination for breach by the Introducer, no commission tail is due. Commissions relating to amounts collected before the effective date of termination remain payable.

### 15.6 Survival

Articles 6.3, 11.3, 12, 13, 14, 15.4, 17 and 18 survive termination of this Agreement.
